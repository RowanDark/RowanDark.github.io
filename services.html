Services

penetration testing and security assessment, scoped to fit

Every engagement follows the Rosec methodology: scoped and authorized in writing, tested by hand, and delivered with a report a non-technical stakeholder can act on. Baseline scope is web application and network testing; wireless, physical, social engineering, and cloud configuration review are available as add-on modules. See Methodology for how an engagement runs end to end.

Web Application Penetration Testing

Manual, tool-assisted testing of a web application against the OWASP Web Security Testing Guide (WSTG) v4.2. Both unauthenticated and authenticated testing are performed, so findings behind a login are covered, not just the public surface. Emphasis is on business logic flaws and access control failures — the class of bug automated scanners consistently miss. You get a findings report scoped to the application's roles and workflows, with reproduction steps for every confirmed issue.

Network Penetration Testing

Testing of external and internal network perimeters, aligned to NIST SP 800-115. Coverage includes service enumeration, exposed configuration issues, and patch-level exposure across in-scope hosts. External testing assesses what's reachable from the internet; internal testing assesses what's reachable from inside your network, including lateral movement and segmentation gaps. You get a findings report mapped to the hosts and services tested.

External Attack Surface Assessment

Passive discovery of your internet-facing footprint — certificate transparency logs, DNS enumeration, and related open-source sources — to surface shadow IT and forgotten subdomains before someone else finds them. This assessment is non-intrusive: it does not touch discovered assets beyond passive lookups, requires no testing window, and causes no downtime. You get an inventory of discovered assets with an assessment of what's exposed and why it matters.

Remediation Retest

Validation that fixes from a prior engagement actually closed the reported findings, rather than just changed their symptoms. Each finding from the original report is retested individually and delivered against a retest tracker showing per-finding pass/fail status, so you have a clear record of what's fixed and what isn't.

Optional modules

The following are scoped as add-ons to a baseline network or web application engagement, not sold as standalone headline services:

  • Wireless network testing
  • Physical security testing
  • Social engineering (phishing and pretexting scenarios)
  • Cloud configuration review

How scoping works

Engagements are scoped starting from a full black-box baseline and scaled down to fit your environment, constraints, and budget. Nothing runs against a target until scope, testing windows, and rules of engagement are authorized in writing.
