Services

penetration testing and security assessment for small and mid-sized businesses

Rosec Cyber LLC performs hands-on offensive security engagements sized for organizations that don't have an in-house security team. Every engagement is scoped up front, run against a written rules of engagement, and delivered with a report a non-security stakeholder can act on.

External network testing

Assessment of internet-facing infrastructure — exposed services, misconfigurations, and known vulnerabilities reachable from outside your network.

Web application testing

Manual and tool-assisted testing of custom and third-party web applications for the flaws automated scanners miss — auth, business logic, and session handling included.

Internal network testing

Simulated internal foothold assessment covering lateral movement, privilege escalation paths, and segmentation gaps.

Security assessment & review

Configuration review and risk assessment for cloud environments, endpoints, and network architecture — for teams that need a baseline before their next audit or insurance renewal.

Every engagement ends with a written report: findings ranked by severity, reproduction steps, and remediation guidance. See Methodology for how engagements are run, or get in touch to scope one.
