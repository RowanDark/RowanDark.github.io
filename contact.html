To scope an engagement or ask a question, reach out by email. There's no contact form on this site — GitHub Pages serves static files only, so email is the direct line.

What to include in a first email

A first email doesn't need to be formal, but the more of this it answers, the more useful the reply can be:

  • What you want tested — application, network, or both
  • Roughly how large the environment is (hosts, apps, or endpoints in scope)
  • Whether there's a compliance deadline driving the engagement, and which framework
  • Your timeline — when you need testing done and a report delivered

How an engagement starts

Every engagement moves through the same three steps before any testing begins:

  1. 01

    Scoping call

    A conversation to understand what you need tested and what a realistic engagement looks like.

  2. 02

    Written SOW

    A statement of work with rules of engagement and a signed authorization letter, covering targets, testing windows, and exclusions.

  3. 03

    Testing begins

    Nothing is tested against your systems until all of the above is signed.
