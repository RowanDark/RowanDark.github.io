How an engagement runs

five steps, start to finish

Every engagement begins with a signed statement of work, a rules of engagement, and an authorization letter. Nothing is tested against your systems until all three are in place.

  1. 01

    Scoping and authorization

    Targets, testing windows, and exclusions are agreed in writing, along with escalation contacts and an emergency stop procedure.

  2. 02

    Reconnaissance

    Passive and active enumeration of the in-scope systems to find out what's actually reachable before any testing begins.

  3. 03

    Testing

    Manual testing, supported by tooling, against the mapped attack surface. Every finding is validated by hand before it's written up.

  4. 04

    Reporting

    Findings are ranked by severity with reproduction steps and remediation guidance, written for both technical staff and the people who sign off on fixing them.

  5. 05

    Retest

    Once fixes are in place, a follow-up test confirms each finding is actually closed.
