Methodology

how an engagement runs, start to finish

Every engagement follows the same structure, regardless of scope. Nothing runs against a target without a signed rules of engagement.

1. Scoping & rules of engagement

Targets, testing windows, and any exclusions are agreed in writing before any testing begins. This is also where escalation contacts and emergency stop procedures are set.

2. Reconnaissance

Passive and active enumeration of the in-scope attack surface — exposed hosts, services, subdomains, and technologies — to build an accurate map of what's actually reachable.

3. Testing & exploitation

Manual testing, supported by tooling, against the mapped surface. Findings are validated before being reported — no unverified scanner output makes it into a report.

4. Reporting

Findings are ranked by severity with clear reproduction steps and remediation guidance, written for both technical staff and non-technical stakeholders.

5. Remediation support

A follow-up retest is available once fixes are in place, to confirm findings are actually closed.
