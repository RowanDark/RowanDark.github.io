About

Covington, Georgia

Rosec Cyber LLC is a Covington, Georgia security consultancy focused on penetration testing and security assessment for small and mid-sized businesses — organizations that need real offensive security work without the overhead of a large firm.

The tooling used during engagements is built and maintained in-house — see Tools for the open-source projects behind the recon and testing work, or Work for output samples.

Notes on tooling, findings, and the security research behind it are published on the Gray Box Substack.

Get in touch to discuss an engagement.
