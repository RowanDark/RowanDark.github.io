I'm the principal security consultant and owner of Rosec Cyber LLC, a penetration testing and security assessment practice based in Covington, Georgia. When you hire a small firm, you're hiring a person, not a logo — this page is about the person actually doing the testing.

Before moving into offensive security, I spent 15+ years in IT operations, systems management, and logistics and operations leadership. That background shapes how I test: I've been the person on the other end of a vague finding with no reproduction steps, so every report I deliver is written to be understood and actioned by the team that has to fix it, not just the person who reads it once. Alongside Rosec, I currently work as an IT project lead and serve as a Microsoft Dynamics 365 security subject-matter expert, which keeps me close to how real business systems are actually configured, permissioned, and broken.

Gray Box is a cybersecurity podcast and Substack I write and record, aimed at translating threat intelligence into something a small business owner can actually use — without the jargon or the assumption that you have a security team.
